PAL mark explorations

Eight directions for the pal mark, each shown at 96, 32 and 16px on paper and ink, plus the Runtime variant of the winner. Scored 1–5 on: 16px (survives as a favicon), mono (works in one color), ownable (not a stock glyph), story (a layer that holds a moment for you) and motion (has a natural animation).

Verdict: 1, Held

The p is the first letter of the name, so the mark and the wordmark are the same drawing: no separate symbol to learn. Its bowl holds a dot, which is the product in one shape (a layer that keeps a moment for you and asks before letting it go) and a nod to पल, "a moment". It stays a p at 16px, works in one ink, and animates without inventing anything: the bowl draws, the dot drops in and settles. 6 (प) has the best story but loses non-Hindi readers at small sizes; it lives on as the origin line in the guide. The Runtime sub-mark (9) keeps the same p and squares the bowl into a container: same family, machine register.
