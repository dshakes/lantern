pal is a **personal agent** for your own iMessage and WhatsApp. Two small programs on your Mac, the **channel bridges**, read new messages and ask a PAL control plane for a reply through the **same sessions API your own agents use**. Every reply a contact would receive passes a **check stack** first. Most messages need a quick, ordinary answer, and those go out after a human-paced delay. A reply that **commits you** to money or an action, or states something pal can't verify, is **held** and shown to you instead.

## Who does what

| | You | pal | The platform |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owns | Your Mac, your accounts, the owner profile | Nothing; it acts as you, on your Mac | Sessions, memory, model routing |
| Does | Teach it, approve held replies, mute or stop it | Drafts, checks, paces and sends ordinary replies | One session per contact, the `web_search` tool, the person graph |
| Sees | Every held reply, plus FYIs in your self-chat | Your threads, your profile, your calendar and mail on the Mac | What the bridge sends it |

You talk to pal in your **self-chat** (a message to yourself, or a dedicated bot account you DM). Both bridges accept commands only from you; a contact's message never reaches the document or action pipeline.

## What happens to one message

1. **It arrives.** The iMessage bridge reads `chat.db` (including RCS and newer messages whose text lives in `attributedBody`); the WhatsApp bridge holds the WhatsApp session.
2. **The bridge decides whether to act.** The killswitch stops everything. Mute means "don't send": pal still drafts and hands you each reply instead. Quiet hours (01:00 to 06:00 by default) queue messages and replay them in the morning.
3. **The control plane drafts.** Each contact has its own session. Side calls (gate judges, voice refinement, ranking) use a **purpose-keyed session** such as `<contact>::refine`, so they can't leak into the contact's live thread. The bridge asks for `model: "auto"` and the model router picks the concrete model.
4. **The check stack runs.** It catches stock phrases and replies that don't sound like you, holds money, promises and unverifiable claims, and rewrites "I sent it" into "I'll send it" unless the bridge really did. [Holds and trust](/docs/pal/human-in-the-loop/) covers each check.
5. **It is sent, or held.** Sent replies wait a delay drawn from your real reply times to that contact and are logged, encrypted, on the Mac. Held replies are paged to your self-chat.

## Limits

- The bridges run on one Mac. If it is off, nothing is processed.
- The grounding gate holds claims it cannot verify; it does not verify them. A scheduled refresh updates what pal knows about you from your mail and calendar, so there is a lag.
- The voice score is a stylometric signal, not a judgment of tone or meaning.
- Voice cloning (`PAL_VOICE_CLONE`) is off by default and treated as deepfake-class.

## Configuration

`pal setup` sets most of this for you. The variables live in the bridge environment:

| Variable | Default | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PAL_OWNER_NAME` | required | Your first name, used for document ranking |
| `PAL_OWNER_TIMEZONE` | process timezone | Quiet hours, digests, calendar lookups |
| `PAL_DRAFT_CONFIRM` (iMessage) / `PAL_DRAFT_HIGH_STAKES` (WhatsApp) | off | Hold every LOW-confidence reply for approval |
| `PAL_VOICE_REFINE` | on | Critique-refine for MEDIUM and LOW drafts |
| `PAL_VOICE_FLOOR` | on | The voice floor; `0` disables |
| `PAL_QUIET_START` / `PAL_QUIET_END` | `1` / `6` | Quiet-hours window, 24-hour clock |

Installing and pairing the bridges: [BOT-SETUP.md](repo:/blob/master/docs/personal/BOT-SETUP.md). Every variable: [Environment variables](/docs/reference/env-vars/).

## Where it lives

The shared logic is in `packages/bridge-core/src/` (`natural.ts`, `voice-score.ts`, `voice-refine.ts`, `commitment-gate.ts`, `grounding-gate.ts`, `verifiable-claims.ts`, `sent-log.ts`, `secure-store.ts`); the iMessage wiring is `services/imessage-bridge/src/session.ts`. `bridge-parity.test.ts` fails the build when a module is wired into one bridge but not the other. Deeper: [pal internals](/docs/architecture/pal/).
