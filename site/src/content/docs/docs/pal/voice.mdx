pal drafts replies in your voice, then measures the draft against **your own sent messages** before it goes out. Three checks are about voice: a **bot-tell guard**, a **voice floor** and a **critique-refine** pass. They are code, not prompt advice, so a draft that reads like a bot is rewritten or dropped rather than sent. You teach pal in three ways: during `pal setup`, by tapping thumbs-down on a bad reply, and by telling it facts in your own chat.

## The checks that protect your voice

These sit inside the full reply pipeline, which also holds money and unverifiable claims: see [Holds and trust](/docs/pal/human-in-the-loop/). A failure here triggers one regeneration, not a hold.

| Check | What it looks at | On failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bot-tell guard | Stock phrases, leaked reasoning, denials of your own facts, and a reply that repeats the shape of your last three replies to that contact | Regenerate once |
| Voice floor | Stylometric distance from your own sent messages | Regenerate once, with a hint about which feature drifted |
| Critique-refine | Medium and low confidence drafts only: edited against the 5 real messages of yours most like it | Keep the original if the edit is not closer to your voice |

After these, the reply waits a human-paced delay drawn from your real reply times to that contact, jittered and clamped to between 3 and 25 seconds.

### How the voice floor works

The floor is fitted on your own sent messages, in memory, and needs at least 50 of them. A draft further from your style than 95% of your real messages is suppressed as `voice-drift`. Every contact draft logs `voice score {delta, floor, ok}`, so you can see it working.

It scores surface style, not meaning. It never runs on your own chat, on groups, or on one- and two-word replies.

## Teach it during setup

Step 3 of [`pal setup`](/docs/pal/surfaces/) can read your recent one-to-one messages, only if you agree at step 1. It shows a profile (register, sentence length, warmth, patterns) and a sample reply as you. Answer `a` if it sounds like you, `r` to say what is off and try again, or `s` to skip. With `pal setup --apply`, an approved profile sets `PAL_VOICE_REASONING=1`, which makes pal use that reasoned profile instead of a fixed heuristic.

## Teach it with a thumbs-down

1. Tap 👎 on a reply. The inbound message, the bad reply and your better reply (if you send one) are logged.
2. Patterns that recur across three or more rejections become **style lessons**, written to the `## Style lessons (managed)` section of your owner profile.
3. Lessons go into every future prompt, for every contact.

A model pass also finds patterns the fixed rules miss. It can only add or raise a lesson, never remove one, and any error falls back to the fixed rules. To retire a lesson, delete its bullet from the profile.

## Tell it facts

Your owner profile (`~/.pal/owner-profile.md`, or the path in `PAL_OWNER_PROFILE`) holds facts pal must never contradict and per-contact naming rules:

```markdown
## Facts
- married: yes
- spouse: Alex
- anniversary: 2015-05-02

## Relationships
- Jordan: college friend | address as: Jo | never: buddy, bro
```

Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`. You can also teach it in your own chat: "remember: anniversary is May 2 2015" or "don't call Jordan buddy". pal answers `📝 noted — …`. `address as:` sets what to call someone; `never:` lists terms pal must not use for them.

## Settings

| Variable | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PAL_VOICE_FLOOR` | on | `0` disables the stylometric floor |
| `PAL_VOICE_REFINE` | on | `0` disables critique-refine |
| `PAL_DISLIKE_LLM_CLUSTER` | on | `0` keeps only the fixed-rule lesson miner |
| `PAL_VOICE_CLONE` | off | Opt-in and deepfake-class. `1` speaks outbound phone calls in a cloned voice (ElevenLabs) instead of a stock one. It needs `PAL_ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`, `PAL_ELEVENLABS_VOICE_ID` and a public cache URL, or it falls back to the stock voice. The recording-consent announcement still plays. |

The remaining voice variables are in [Environment variables](/docs/reference/env-vars/) and [pal internals](/docs/architecture/pal/).
