pal reads your messages, the subjects of your mail and your calendar, so you should know where that goes and how to stop it. pal acts **only for you**, keeps its state **on your Mac**, and has two off switches: **mute** and the **kill switch**. Setup asks for your consent before it reads anything; see [Connect your channels](/docs/pal/surfaces/).

## Who it acts for

Only you. Document search, Mac actions (Calendar, Notes, Mail) and every command run only in your own chat. A message from anyone else never reaches them. That works with one account messaging itself, or with a separate bot account you message.

## Your switches

| You want | Do this | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stop it sending | `mute` | pal still drafts every reply, but sends each one to you instead of the contact. If handing it to you fails, the reply is dropped, never sent. |
| Stop it entirely | `kill switch on` | All inbound is ignored until you release it. |
| Approve risky replies first | `PAL_DRAFT_CONFIRM=on` (iMessage) / `PAL_DRAFT_HIGH_STAKES=on` (WhatsApp) | Low-confidence replies wait for your `send`. Off by default. |
| Quiet nights | `PAL_QUIET_START` / `PAL_QUIET_END` (default 1 and 6) | Messages queue and are answered in the morning. |

Mute is enforced where messages leave the bridge: while muted, every destination other than you is blocked, including groups and follow-ups. Unmuting does not answer messages that arrived while muted.

Money and unverifiable claims are [always held](/docs/pal/human-in-the-loop/), whatever these switches say.

## Where your data lives

| Where | What | Protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your Mac, `~/.pal/` | pal's state: profile, episodes, topics, dislikes, the log of sent replies, OCR cache | Owner-only (mode 0600). The stores with the most personal detail (episodes, topics, dislikes, the sent log) are also AES-256-GCM encrypted, with a key created beside them. |
| Your control plane | The person graph and timeline that link iMessage and WhatsApp | Row-level security by tenant |
| Your model provider | The message text sent for each reply | Nothing else leaves the Mac |

## What it can read

| Source | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| Documents | Only inside `PAL_PERSONAL_DOCS_ROOTS`. OCR results are cached owner-only. |
| Mail | Sender, subject and date from Apple Mail's local index, read-only. `PAL_MAIL_INDEX=0` turns it off. |
| App usage | Off unless `PAL_MAC_USAGE=on`. Only a one-line daily summary is kept, and it is never used in a reply to a contact. |

## No silent drops

Every reply pal decides not to send logs which check stopped it and why. Mute and the kill switch are counted in a periodic `LIVENESS` log line, so "pal is quiet" always has a reason you can find.
